# Academic citation

[CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) provides machine-readable software citation metadata.
A plain-text reference is:

> RXY712200. *LayerKeySort* [Computer software]. GitHub.
> https://github.com/RXY712200/LayerKeySort

For reproducibility, identify the component and exact commit used, and link to
that immutable source. Describe relevant configuration, modifications and
experimental artifacts in the methods or artifact record. Keep the generic
project citation separate from those study-specific details.

Software citation credits the implementation. A research publication should be
cited for the particular method, result or evidence used from that publication.
Future publications must be associated with their specific research scope and
software revisions; a study of one component or revision does not represent the
entire project. Publication authorship is established for each work, independently
of the repository's copyright notice and contributor history.

Academic citation is requested as scholarly credit, not as an additional license
condition. The [MIT License](LICENSE) remains the sole permission notice here;
its notice-preservation obligations are explained in [COPYRIGHT.md](COPYRIGHT.md).
No additional approval, citation, noncommercial-use or publication restriction is
imposed on MIT permissions.
