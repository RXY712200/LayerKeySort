### C. Candidate scope and falsifiable designs

## Step 2/7 extension — implement, measure, and decide

### A. Pre-measurement screening and adoption rules

Before new candidate timing, commit the protocol with fixed trace set, priorities, thresholds, resource/tail budgets, selection process and stop criteria. Compare against **accepted Preview.1**, not just weaker 4.1.0. Default meaningful incremental objective: at least 20% median elapsed reduction for comparison-heavy and 8% overall against Preview.1 on the eventual contemporaneous two-host matrix, or another prospectively justified compelling tradeoff (e.g. materially better target-domain comparison and parity outside it with bounded extra memory/tails). Treat as candidate selection objectives, not an unconditional requirement to adopt. Prespecify the exact exception/decision rule before candidate results; do not move goalposts after seeing them. Quantify gap to both classical OM adapters and number of conditions won. No requirement for universal wins or exact equal memory. Use measured baseline variability and real target workload priorities. Provisional planning allowance: up to approximately 15% extra requested live/peak representation storage in affected meaningful target cells may be justified by a substantial gain; a larger increase requires a specific pre-result resource justification and owner review before product adoption, not automatic rejection of research. Set separate tail and worst-case work budgets prospectively; retain all adverse cells, systematic regressions and significant tail increases. No hidden >10% systematic non-target slowdown outside noise, unbounded relabel pauses or unacceptable sparse retention without a documented, explicitly justified decision. Correctness/compatibility/failure guarantees are absolute under the existing contract. Keep the previous 15% overall /25% compare-heavy improvement against v4.1.0 as a reference and avoid surrendering the accepted core gains.

### B. Prototype screening, ablation, adversarial tests

First use a bounded selection subset of existing and preselected independent states; only execute the full matrix on a viable final candidate and required contemporaneous controls. Establish fair public external-call boundaries, separate timing from diagnostics; compare simple fast path, maintenance path and overall operation mix. Retain actual measurements even on rejected prototypes. Test repeated narrow-gap insertion long enough to exceed the previous 1,057-event failure, persistent hotspot, front/back growth, randomized fragmented and churn states, splits/merges/redistributions at varied block occupancies, reversal/long moves, comparison across hierarchy, restore/bulk builds and shrink/regrow. Explicitly record label exhaustion/relabel/rebuild/fallback counts and affected blocks/bytes, max and distribution of writes, operation p50/p95/p99/p99.9/max where clock resolution and samples support them; avoid pretending timer floor is precision. Measure comparison-only, update/move-heavy, managed insertion/search, allocation, cache/index access and resource effects; confirm that label speed does not simply transfer prohibitive costs into mutations. Track independent expected sequence and all valid handle identities after actual structural changes, not just append-built fixed states. Include fault injection at reached allocations and at any newly fallible preparation. Test saturation/overflow and fail-safe transitions without externally invalidating handles, revisions, immutable snapshots or history. Compare ablations to isolate any separate metadata/update optimizations.
