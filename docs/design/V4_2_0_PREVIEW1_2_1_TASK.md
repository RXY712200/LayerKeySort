### 1.2 Investigate five specific candidate directions

The following are research directions, not five mandatory production changes. Examine actual callers/invariants and prior negative evidence. Screen and prioritize by measured cost, expected gain, semantic risk, maintenance cost and feasible independent evaluation; reject low-value hypotheses early with reasons.

1. **Dynamic block maintenance (highest priority).** Examine shifted suffix maintenance already optimized in Preview.1, actual pointer moves and repair loops in ordinary insertion/removal, cross-block relocation, splits, merges, redistributions and source-underoccupancy. Investigate avoidable writes and array copies, local transfer strategies, occupancy-aware split/repair policy and when one can retain a block without new allocation. Do not weaken committed occupancy/identity or require additional allocation for previously allocation-free operations. Evaluate locality, adversarial hotspot and alternating move/churn, not just append.
2. **AVL update/index path.** Profile height, parent links, balance, subtree counts, rotations, managed search and bulk restore. Identify unnecessary refresh propagation or redundant metadata reads/writes. Retain accurate tree invariants and root/parent relationships. The previous subtree-count-only ablation was flat overall and tests still read it; do not mechanically repeat removal without a materially different hypothesis or data. No speculative rewrite of a now-fast comparison path.
3. **Long-run label health.** Current nonzero 64-bit tags increase strictly along the threaded list; zero uses the logarithmic AVL fallback. Maintenance at attachment probes at most 64 block widths and writes at most 64 tags; successful overlapping repair may recover zero labels but no guaranteed reclamation. Quantify actual frequency and cumulative cost of fallback, local redistribution, zero-tag persistence/clustering, post-deletion recovery and fast-path prevalence across realistic long-running states. Investigate bounded relabel/recovery strategies, better gap/density choice or adaptive local repair only if there is measured value. No global stop-the-world relabel, new recoverable failure point or unbounded retry hidden in ordinary operations. Never expose tags as persistent identifiers or serialize them into LS1/wire. Preserve the fallback and demonstrate its correctness.
4. **Block capacity/fill and cache tradeoff.** B64 was selected under a different cost structure. Compare the current default against a small, justified range of compile-time capacity candidates (e.g. 32/64/128, plus 16 or 96 only if justified). Use the actual private even-capacity policy and adapt tests that intentionally assume the default rather than silently skipping them. Measure occupancy distributions, number of blocks, shifts, structural events, tag maintenance, compare locality, memory and tail. Test sparse/churn and restored shapes, scale and managed search, not append-only. Capacity is private; preserve public format and semantics. A single faster capacity on one trace is not grounds to change the default. Keep B64 unless integrated data clearly supports a replacement. Do not make new public tuning parameters without authorization.
5. **Handle allocation/access and representation.** Profile per-resident allocation and allocator/cache locality; explore low-risk reductions of duplicate loads, metadata or call overhead. Revisit a pool/slab or changed private representation only if measured allocation costs justify and there is a credible path around the old sparse-retention failure. Stable live Handle addresses, partial allocation failure atomicity, reclamation after mass deletion and callback/lifetime rules are non-negotiable. Do not revive the old 64-slot slab without testing adversarial survivors (historical 1m to 1% remaining), requested retained and peak resources, and cleanup. No allocator replacement simply for benchmark advantage or new public runtime dependency.

## Step 2/7 extension — integrate, validate, measure ceiling and choose

### 2.1 Prespecified engineering objectives and stopping rules

The purpose is incremental value over accepted Preview.1.2, not chasing an artificially weak baseline. Before looking at new candidate measurements, use the following initial decision rule or write a technically justified alternative prospectively in the committed protocol. Do not change acceptance thresholds after seeing the results.

Default adoption performance criterion on BOTH native Windows and Linux:
- Route A: at least 5% median elapsed reduction over Preview.1.2 across all 45 frozen cells per host, or
- Route B: at least 10% median elapsed reduction in at least two of update-heavy, hotspot, long moves (each nine cells per host) plus at least 2% median reduction across all 45 cells on each host. The two target families must be the same across hosts. These are deliberately incremental thresholds after the large prior gains.
- In either route, preserve the earlier Preview.1.2 comparison-heavy advantage: no median deterioration exceeding 5% relative to Preview.1.2 across the compare-heavy subset per host without a separately authorized, prospectively justified tradeoff. Investigate systematic non-target median slowdowns greater than 10% outside variability; isolated adverse cells are reported, not erased. Do not adopt a noisy threshold crossing as meaningful evidence; confirm decision-critical samples with a prospectively documented paired repeat or noise assessment. Report exact arithmetic, cell weights and host variability, not pooled host time.
- A compelling maintenance/resource-only outcome (e.g. demonstrated severe saturation improvement with negligible timing change or materially reduced memory) can be a valid research result; if it fails A/B, report a separate supported adoption recommendation, not a retroactive pass, and request owner decision before treating it as the selected version.

Default resource budget: at most 5% added requested live/peak representation storage relative to Preview.1.2 in primary cells, unless protocol gives a specific justified higher budget (up to 15% for substantial measured gains); outside that allowance requires explicit owner decision before adoption. Measure large/churn retention separately, including allocation traffic and RSS where supported; avoid sacrificing the 4.1 memory advantage for a marginal speed win. No guaranteed zero growth. Set explicit adverse-tail guard before timing (default no recurring family median p99.9 above 1.5x control outside resolution/noise, no unexplained repeated large pauses and no regression in actual bounded maintenance guarantee). A different bounded tradeoff must be justified prospectively. Compare fallback prevalence and costs against exact Preview.1.2; do not equate zero tag fallbacks in a single trace with general guarantees. All correctness, recoverable failure and frozen compatibility are absolute; no post-hoc fixture edits. No forced adoption. No extra exploration after completing this bounded cycle.
